FAQ

Fair questions.

What am I backing exactly?
A live predictive block-template engine for Bitcoin mining running on public mainnet. Audit data is open. This crowdfunding round covers the infrastructure hardening for the open-onboarding window (redundant Sphere nodes, upstream bandwidth, and the audit-log endpoint), not equity, not a token, not a promise of future returns.
How does a hashrate pledge work?
You send us a rough figure and a way to reach you. When the onboarding window opens (~3-4 weeks from now) we ping you first with the pool-side connection details and the compensation model. No obligation to actually connect. If the terms don't fit you're out with zero friction.
What if I want my money back?
30-day no-questions refund on both rails, BTC and Fiat, same channel you contributed on. Email sales@preblock.io and it's processed the same day.
Will my name be listed?
Only if you explicitly say yes. Default is anonymous ("one operator has pledged ~1 PH/s"). We always show the exact wording to you before it goes live.
When does open onboarding actually start?
The 3-4 week window is our current best estimate. If it slips, it slips on this page first — with the reason and the revised date. No silent delays.
